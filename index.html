Sage Tab transforms Chrome's blank new tab into a complete, customizable workspace — modular widgets, smart alarms, bookmarks, an ad-free news reader, focus tools, and ambient soundscapes, all kept private on your device.

No server of ours

Sage Tab has no backend. Your widgets, notes, and settings live in Chrome's own storage on your device, synced only through your own Google Account if you enable it.

Nothing sold, nothing tracked

No ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers are bundled with Sage Tab. We don't know who you are, and we don't want to.

Why Sage Tab asks for what it asks for

Permissions, in plain language

Chrome requires an extension to declare everything it might touch up front. Here's what each one is actually for.

storage

Saves your widgets, layout, notes, tasks, and settings locally — and through your Google Account if Chrome sync is on.

bookmarks

Powers the Bookmarks drawer and Speed Dial widget, so you can browse and launch your existing Chrome bookmarks from the new tab page.

tabs / tabGroups / sessions

Lets dashboard widgets show, search, and jump to your open tabs, tab groups, and recently closed sessions.

alarms

Schedules a periodic background refresh of the news feeds you've subscribed to, so new articles are ready when you open a tab.

identity

Reads your signed-in Chrome profile's name for the header greeting and to key your synced data to your account. Never used to sign you into anything else, and nothing is sent off your device.

host permissions

A fixed list of news, weather, and search domains, contacted directly from your browser only to fetch the content those widgets display — never through a server we run.

optional: any site

Requested only at the moment you add a custom RSS feed from a site outside the built-in list, and only for that action. You can decline it.
